CTVET Diploma

Nationally recognised higher-level technical qualification

2-Year Programme
 Entry Requirements
  • 1 Five (5) SSCE / WASSCE passes including English Language and Mathematics or
  • 2 Five New Certificate 2 passes including English Language and Mathematics or
  • 3 Five (5) Old Certificate 2 (GBCE) passes including English Language and Mathematics or
  • 4 A one-year certificate in a relevant area from any accredited institution

Certificate of Competence

Skills-based certification for working professionals

1 or 2 Year Programme
 Entry Requirements
  • 1 A certificate from JHS, SSCE, SSSCE or WASSCE or
  • 2 At least one (1) year of relevant working experience in a related field or
  • 3 Must be at least 25 years of age at time of application or
  • 4 Must provide proof of date of birth with a valid National ID Card
