Got Questions?

Frequently
asked.

Can't find what you need? Contact us on WhatsApp at +233 54 462 2250 or visit the admissions office.

Do I need an e-voucher to apply?
No e-voucher needed! GH Schools accepts direct payment through our secure online portal. Simply apply, pay GHC 200 online, and you're all set.
How much is the application fee?
The application fee is GHC 200, paid securely through our online portal. No additional charges or bank visits required.
Can I edit my application after submitting?
Yes! You can revisit and edit your application before the deadline. Simply log back into the portal with your credentials to make changes.
What if I have D7 in English or Maths?
You may still be considered for our COC or Diploma programs. We encourage you to apply — our admissions team will personally assess your eligibility.
What programs does GH Schools offer?
GH Schools offers the 1-Year COC Certificate and 2-Year CTVET Diploma across 5 schools: Media, Fashion, Catering, Cosmetology, and Technology. Each program is hands-on and career-focused.
Having trouble logging in?
Contact our support team on WhatsApp at +233 54 462 2250 or visit the admissions office directly for quick assistance.
